The little details

Frequently asked questions.

What does the 1-year warranty cover?⌄

Electros products include a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects, starting from the delivery date. Accidental damage and normal wear are not covered. Keep your order confirmation as proof of purchase and raise a Warranty Claim if you need help.

Can I request a replacement within 7 days?⌄

If an item arrives damaged or has a manufacturing defect, raise a request within 7 days of delivery. Keep the product and packaging available while the request is reviewed. Replacement is subject to inspection and stock availability.

How long do UPI refunds take?⌄

After a refund is approved, it is sent to the original payment method. UPI refunds typically appear within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider. This storefront uses simulated payments, so no real payment or refund is processed.

When should my order arrive?⌄

Most orders are expected to arrive within 3–7 business days after dispatch. Delivery times vary by destination and service availability. Use the Order ID above for the latest saved status; live carrier tracking is not connected in this demo.

Where can I find my Order ID?⌄

Your Order ID appears on the confirmation screen after checkout and in My Orders on your profile page. You can paste it into the tracker with or without a leading #.
